Questions

Faucet & Shower System Installation FAQ

How much does a custom shower system cost?

It depends on the valve, the number of outlets (rainfall, handheld, body jets), and the finish. A simple upgrade is modest; a multi-function spa shower is a larger investment. We quote a fixed price after reviewing your plumbing.

What's the difference between thermostatic and pressure-balancing valves?

Pressure-balancing valves keep temperature steady when water is used elsewhere in the home; thermostatic valves let you set an exact temperature and run multiple outlets at once. We'll recommend the right one for your setup.

Can I mix metal finishes?

Yes, tastefully. Many designs pair a warm-metal faucet with complementary hardware. We help you coordinate finishes so the look is intentional rather than mismatched.

Do you move plumbing for a new faucet or shower layout?

Yes. We can relocate supply lines and valves as part of a remodel, handling the rough-in, waterproofing, and finish work to code.

How long does a shower system installation take?

A simple fixture swap can be same-day; a new custom system within a remodel is part of the overall one-to-four-week project timeline.
