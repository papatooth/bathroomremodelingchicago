Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Chicago?

Most projects range from $12,000 for a straightforward update to $40,000+ for a full luxury master bath. Costs depend on size, materials, and scope. We provide a free, detailed, fixed-price quote so you know exactly what you'll pay.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

A typical full remodel takes 2–4 weeks. Smaller updates or shower conversions can often be completed in about a week. We give you a clear timeline before we start.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are fully licensed, bonded, and insured in Illinois and pull all required City of Chicago permits for your project.

Do you offer financing?

Yes — flexible financing options are available for qualified homeowners. Ask about it during your free consultation.

Do you offer a warranty?

Absolutely. Every project is backed by our 5-year workmanship warranty, plus manufacturer warranties on materials and fixtures.
