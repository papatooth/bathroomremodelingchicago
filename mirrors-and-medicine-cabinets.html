Questions

Mirror & Medicine Cabinet Installation FAQ

Are backlit LED mirrors worth it?

They provide even, shadow-free light that's ideal for grooming and makeup, plus a modern glow. Many include dimming and anti-fog — a popular, high-impact upgrade for Chicago bathrooms.

Can a medicine cabinet be recessed into the wall?

Yes, when the wall cavity allows. Recessed cabinets sit flush for a built-in look; we verify framing, plumbing, and wiring before cutting in.

Do lighted mirrors need an electrician?

They require a proper power connection, which we handle to code as part of the installation.

What size mirror do I need?

Generally a bit narrower than your vanity and centered over the sink. For double vanities, two mirrors or one wide unit both work — we'll advise on the best look.

Can you add outlets inside a medicine cabinet?

Many models offer interior outlets and USB charging to keep the counter clutter-free, and we can install them.
