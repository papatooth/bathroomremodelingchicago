Questions

Toilet Installation FAQ

How much does it cost to install a new toilet in Chicago?

A straightforward replacement typically runs a few hundred dollars plus the fixture, while wall-hung and smart toilets cost more due to in-wall carriers, electrical, and added plumbing. We always provide a clear, fixed-price quote before any work begins.

Are smart toilets worth it?

For many homeowners, yes. Heated seats, warm-water bidet washing, automatic lids, and self-cleaning features add real daily comfort and hygiene. We'll help you weigh the features against your budget.

What is a wall-hung toilet?

It's a wall-mounted bowl with the tank concealed inside the wall. It frees up floor space, makes cleaning easier, and gives a modern, minimalist look — ideal for powder rooms and contemporary baths.

Do you remove and dispose of my old toilet?

Yes. Removal, haul-away, and clean installation of the new fixture are all included in our service.

Can you install a toilet as part of a full remodel?

Absolutely. We handle complete bathroom remodels — plumbing, tile, vanities, lighting, and fixtures — so your new toilet is one coordinated piece of the finished room.
