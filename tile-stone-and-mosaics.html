Tile and stone define the character of a bathroom more than any other surface. Questions

Tile & Stone Installation FAQ

How much does bathroom tile installation cost in Chicago?

Cost depends on the material, square footage, and complexity — mosaics and natural stone take more labor than standard tile. We provide a detailed, fixed-price quote after reviewing your project.

Is natural stone or porcelain better for a bathroom?

Porcelain is extremely durable and low-maintenance; natural stone offers unmatched beauty but needs periodic sealing and gentle care. We'll help you choose based on your lifestyle and upkeep preferences.

What is a curbless shower?

A barrier-free shower with no step to enter — sleek, modern, and excellent for accessibility. It requires careful floor sloping and waterproofing, which we specialize in.

Can you add a heated floor?

Yes. Electric radiant heat under tile is a popular upgrade for Chicago winters, and we install it as part of the tile work.

How do you prevent leaks and mold?

With proper waterproofing membranes, correct slope, quality setting materials, and sealed penetrations — the fundamentals that cut-rate installs often skip.
