Questions

Vanities & Cabinetry FAQ

What's the advantage of a floating vanity?

It frees up visual floor space, makes cleaning easier, and gives a modern, high-end look. It does require solid wall blocking, which we install as part of the job.

Should I get a single or double vanity?

Double sinks are great for shared primary baths if you have the width; a single vanity with more counter and storage often works better in tighter spaces. We'll plan the best fit for your room.

What countertop is best for a bathroom?

Quartz is durable and low-maintenance; natural marble and stone are stunning but need sealing. We'll match the surface to your style and how much upkeep you want.

Can you reuse my existing plumbing?

Often yes, but new vanities sometimes shift the sink location. We adjust supply and drain lines as needed for a clean, correct result.

Do you offer custom vanities?

We offer premium stock and semi-custom cabinetry, and can coordinate custom solutions for unusual or oversized spaces.
