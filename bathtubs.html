Questions

Bathtub Installation FAQ

How much does freestanding tub installation cost in Chicago?

The tub itself ranges widely by brand and material; installation adds plumbing for the filler and drain plus any floor prep. We provide one fixed-price quote covering both the fixture and the labor.

Will my floor support a freestanding tub?

A filled stone or cast tub is heavy. We assess your floor structure and reinforce it if needed so the installation is safe and solid for the long term.

Should I choose a soaking tub or an air bath?

A soaking tub is about deep, quiet immersion; an air bath adds warm-air jets and often chromotherapy for therapeutic relaxation. We'll match the choice to how you like to unwind.

Can you convert my tub to a walk-in shower?

Yes — tub-to-shower conversions (and the reverse) are among our most common requests. We handle the plumbing, waterproofing, tile, and glass.

Where should the tub filler go?

Freestanding floor-mounted and deck-mounted fillers each have advantages. We place it for the best look and ergonomics based on your tub and layout.
