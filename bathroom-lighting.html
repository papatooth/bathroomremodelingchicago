Questions

Bathroom Lighting Installation FAQ

How should bathroom lighting be layered?

Combine task lighting at the mirror, ambient recessed or ceiling light, and optional accent lighting — all dimmable. This flatters grooming and sets a relaxing mood.

Where should vanity lighting go?

Sconces on either side of the mirror at roughly eye level give the most flattering, shadow-free light. Over-mirror fixtures and backlit mirrors also work well.

Can you put lights in the shower?

Yes — using damp- or wet-rated recessed fixtures rated for the location and installed to code.

Do you install dimmers and smart controls?

Absolutely. Dimmers are one of the highest-impact, lowest-cost upgrades, and we can add smart lighting controls too.

Will new lighting require new wiring?

Sometimes. Adding sconces or relocating fixtures may require new runs and boxes, which we handle as licensed professionals.
