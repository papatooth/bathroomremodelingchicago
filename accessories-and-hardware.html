Questions

Accessory & Hardware Installation FAQ

Why does finishing hardware matter so much?

It's what your hands touch every day and what ties the design together. Coordinated, well-placed hardware makes a bathroom feel custom and complete.

Should all my hardware match?

Finishes should coordinate — faucets, lighting, and accessories in complementary metals. Styles can vary slightly, but a consistent finish palette looks most intentional.

Can you install grab bars that look good?

Yes. Modern grab bars come in designer finishes and can double as towel bars, mounted into solid blocking for safety.

Do towel bars need special mounting?

They need secure anchoring — ideally into blocking or studs — so they don't pull loose over time. We ensure a solid install.

Can you match new hardware to my existing fixtures?

We'll coordinate new hardware to your faucets and finishes, or refresh everything for a cohesive, updated look.
